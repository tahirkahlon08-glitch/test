Our Story

Built on a simple belief: a haircut should be an experience, not an errand.

Crown & Co. opened its doors in 2012 with one chair, one barber, and that single belief. Founder Marcus Cole spent a decade cutting hair in downtown Toronto before bringing his craft north to Vaughan — determined to build the kind of shop he'd want to sit in himself: unhurried, detail-obsessed and genuinely welcoming.

Twelve years and fifteen thousand haircuts later, that single chair has grown into one of Vaughan's most trusted barbershops. The tools have been upgraded and the team has grown — but the standard hasn't changed: every guest leaves sharper than they came in.
